Paste a direct video-file link to inspect it and download the original file. Built for mobile, with no account required in this starter.

Quality selector is a preference only in this starter. Direct file links download at their original resolution; choosing 1080p cannot upscale a lower-quality source.

Permission-first

For videos you own, have permission to download, or that the source explicitly makes downloadable.

Platform compatibility: This starter currently accepts direct public video-file URLs (such as links ending in .mp4 or .webm). It does not extract videos from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, private accounts, or DRM-protected streams. Add official APIs or platform adapters only where downloads are permitted by the platform and the rights holder. Never use it to bypass access controls.
